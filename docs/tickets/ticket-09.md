# Ticket 9 — Employee Mail Forwarding

## User

Laura Wilson — Finance Officer

## Request

> "Please forward my incoming email to the Finance shared mailbox so the team can continue receiving messages while my mailbox is being transitioned."

## Investigation

Laura Wilson's mailbox had previously been converted to a shared mailbox as part of the controlled employee-lifecycle workflow.

The forwarding requirement was configured as internal forwarding to the Finance shared mailbox.

The destination was:

finance@apexserviceslab.onmicrosoft.com

No external or personal email address was used for this scenario.

## Forwarding Configuration

Internal email forwarding was configured for Laura Wilson's converted mailbox.

![Laura Email Forwarding Configuration](../images/79%20Laura%20Email%20forwarding%20configuration.png)

Evidence: Internal forwarding configuration for Laura Wilson's mailbox.

## Verification

A test email was sent to Laura's mailbox to verify that the configured forwarding behavior worked.

The Finance shared mailbox was checked to confirm receipt of the forwarded message.

![Laura Forwarding Delivery Test](../images/80%20Laura%20forwarding%20delivery%20test.png)

Evidence: Forwarding delivery test confirming receipt by the Finance shared mailbox.

## Resolution

The requested internal forwarding configuration was successfully configured and verified.

## Technician Resolution

> Configured internal email forwarding from Laura Wilson's mailbox to the Finance shared mailbox and verified successful delivery through a test message.

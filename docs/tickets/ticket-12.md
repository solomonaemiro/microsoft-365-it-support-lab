# Ticket 12 — Conference Room Booking

## User

Jennifer Thomas — Operations Manager

## Request

> "I need to schedule a meeting in Conference Room 1. Please check whether the room is available and book it."

## Investigation

The Conference Room 1 resource mailbox was reviewed to confirm that the room was configured as a bookable resource.

The room was configured as:

- Resource Type: Room
- Capacity: 10
- Location: Main Office

![Conference Room 1 Resource Mailbox](../images/13%20conference%20room%201%20resource%20mailbox.png)

Evidence: Conference Room 1 resource mailbox configuration.

## Booking Test

Conference Room 1 was selected as the meeting location and an actual booking was created through the calendar.

The booking was then checked to verify that the room reservation was accepted.

![Conference Room Booking Test](../images/83%20Conference%20room%20booking%20test.png)

Evidence: Actual Conference Room 1 booking test.

## Resolution

Conference Room 1 was successfully booked through the calendar after confirming that the resource was configured correctly.

## Technician Resolution

> Verified the Conference Room 1 resource mailbox configuration and successfully booked the room through the calendar to confirm that the resource was available and functioning correctly.
